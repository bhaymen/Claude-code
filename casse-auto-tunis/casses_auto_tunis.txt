Updated 2026-11-13
============================================================================
YOUR CAR: Citroën C1 2016 = 2nd generation (2014-2022), 5-door. You need the FRONT RIGHT and REAR RIGHT doors.
MATCHING CARS: Peugeot 108 and Toyota Aygo (2014-2022, 5-door) share the same base and doors. Ask for those too.
DON'T BUY doors from the old C1 / Peugeot 107 / Aygo (2005-2014): they don't fit.
HAVE READY: carte grise (VIN) and your paint colour. ASK: complete door or shell only? Price? Warranty? WhatsApp photos?
SAY: "Bonjour, je cherche la porte avant droite et la porte arrière droite pour une Citroën C1 5 portes 2016 (ou Peugeot 108 / Toyota Aygo 2014+). Vous avez ça ? Quel prix ?"

TIP: El Yahoudia (El Mourouj) has many casse yards next to each other. If the calls don't work out, go there in person.

WARNING: I found these through web search (Facebook, directories). I didn't call any of them, so numbers may be old.
Maps: Casse Auto Agrebi has an exact Google Maps pin. The other links search Google Maps by name and address.
Phones: the tel: links work in most phone apps. The HTML file has tap-to-call buttons.
